HUMANS.md
=========

Technological advances  
Have begotten new romances;  
Some have taken sudden fancy  
To a kind of necromancy.

Neophytes, *non compos mentis*,  
Each a [sorcerer’s apprentice][flooded],  
Skills no greater than a layman,  
Now aspires to be a shaman

Summoning, with vain commands  
Daemons no one understands  
Spending token after token  
Generating code that’s broken

From a cauldron alchemistic  
(Wholly [non-deterministic][entropy])  
Who hath wrought such thaumaturgy?  
Meet the new agentic clergy:

Visionary plans myopic  
Malintentioned, misanthropic

Ransack every online village  
Hack and plunder, scrape and pillage

Feed the whole of human culture  
To the universal mulcher

All your data at the center  
[ABANDON ALL HOPE, YE WHO ENTER][inferno]

*[Apoloimen, mega rhexas][akleios]*  
We have built the [Torment Nexus][torment]!

Don we now our priestly raiment  
Take a bow and take your payment  
Slide your card and [pull the lever][skinner]...

**You’ll depend on us, forever.**

----------------

*Or...*

Take a path beyond the vortex —  
Trust your own cerebral cortex.
